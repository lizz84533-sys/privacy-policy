How do I use the app?

Tap how you're feeling, like Anxiety, Hope, or Sadness. You'll see a Bible story about someone who felt the same way, plus King James verses for that feeling.

How do I save my favorite verses?

Tap Save on any verse. To see them all, tap My Favorites on the home screen. Tap Saved again to remove one.

Your favorites are kept on your phone only. If you delete the app, your favorites are deleted too.

How do I share a verse?

Tap Share on any verse. The app makes a gold picture of the verse, and your phone's share menu opens so you can send it by text, post it, or save it to your Photos.

The Listen button isn't making sound

Turn up your volume and make sure your phone isn't on silent. On iPhone, check the ring/silent switch on the side. If it still doesn't work, close the app and open it again.

Does the app cost anything?

No. The app is free, with no ads and no sign-up. Scripture is from the King James Version, which is in the public domain.
