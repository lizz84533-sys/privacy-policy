Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

This privacy policy applies to all apps published by Liz Rodriguez, including Boss, Bible Verses for Everyday Living, Busy Bee Steps, and any apps published in the future. It explains what information each app may collect, how it's used, and how it's protected.

Boss

Boss is a business organizer app for tracking mileage, receipts, reminders, and notes.

Information collected

  • Your name and login credentials
  • Trip and mileage data, including GPS location when using trip tracking
  • Receipts and expense information you enter
  • Reminders, notes, and calendar events you create
  • If connected, limited access to Google or Outlook calendar and email, used only to sync items you authorize

Data is stored securely using Supabase, tied to your individual account. You can disconnect calendar/email integrations at any time in the app.

Bible Verses for Everyday Living

Bible Verses for Everyday Living is a devotional app that provides verses and uses your device's built-in text-to-speech to read them aloud.

Information collected

This app does not require an account or collect personal information. Any preferences (like saved verses) are stored locally on your device. The Support & Donate button links out to an external donation page (Buy Me a Coffee), which has its own separate privacy policy.

Busy Bee Steps

Busy Bee Steps is a wellness and step-tracking app.

Information collected

This app uses your device's step counter/pedometer sensor to track activity. Step and activity data is stored locally on your device and is not shared with third parties.

Future apps

This policy is also intended to cover any future apps published by Liz Rodriguez. Unless a future app collects meaningfully different or more sensitive information than described here, this same policy applies. If a future app requires different data practices, this page will be updated with a new section describing that app, and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.

How we use your information

Information collected by each app is used only to provide that app's core features to you. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

Data retention and deletion

Data tied to an account (such as a Boss account) remains stored until you delete it or close your account. To request deletion of your account and data, contact us below. Apps that store data locally on your device remove that data when you uninstall the app.

Children's privacy

These apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, including to add new apps. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
