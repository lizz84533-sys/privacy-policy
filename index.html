Privacy Policy for Boss

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Boss ("the app") is a business organizer app built to help you track mileage, receipts, reminders, and notes. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and how it's protected.

Information we collect

When you create an account and use Boss, we may collect:

  • Your name and login credentials (used to identify your account)
  • Trip and mileage data you enter, including GPS location data when you use the trip tracking feature
  • Receipts and related expense information you upload or enter
  • Reminders, notes, and calendar events you create in the app
  • If you choose to connect them, basic account access to your Google or Outlook calendar and email, used only to sync events and messages you authorize

How we use your information

Your information is used only to provide the app's core features to you: storing your business records, calculating mileage deductions, syncing reminders and calendar events, and displaying your receipts and notes back to you. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a third-party backend service. Data is tied to your individual account and is not visible to other users.

Third-party services

If you choose to connect Google Calendar, Outlook Calendar, Gmail, or Outlook Mail, Boss requests only the minimum access needed to sync the specific events or messages you authorize. You can disconnect these integrations at any time from within the app.

Data retention and deletion

Your data remains stored under your account until you choose to delete it or close your account. To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact us using the email below.

Children's privacy

Boss is intended for business use by adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
