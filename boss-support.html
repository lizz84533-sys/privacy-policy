How does GPS mileage tracking work?

Go to Mileage and tap Start GPS Trip. Keep Boss open and on screen during your drive, then tap stop when you arrive. Boss adds up the miles and saves the trip. You can also type in miles by hand.

If GPS doesn't start, go to Settings › Boss › Location and choose While Using the App.

How is my deduction figured?

Boss multiplies your business miles by the IRS standard mileage rate for that year, and charity/volunteer miles by the charity rate. It's an estimate for your records. Boss does not give tax advice, so check with a tax professional.

How much does it cost?

Boss Premium is free for 1 week, then $8.99 per month. It renews automatically each month until you cancel.

How do I cancel?

On iPhone, go to Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Boss › Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before your free week or month ends so you won't be charged again.

I got a new phone. How do I get my subscription back?

Log in to Boss, and on the subscription screen tap Restore Purchase. Use the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

How do I delete my account?

In Boss, tap Log Out, then tap Delete my account and Delete Forever. This permanently deletes your account and everything in it: trips, receipts, reminders, notes and photos. Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription, so cancel that separately in Settings.

Privacy

Your trips, receipts, reminders, notes and photos are saved to your private Boss account so they're there when you switch phones. Boss doesn't sell your information or track you. Read the full privacy policy.
